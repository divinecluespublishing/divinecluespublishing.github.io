Sweep My Email

See who sends you the most email, grouped by company — inbox, spam, and promotions — and stop the junk with working unsubscribe, block, and filter options. When you're done, it even asks if you'd like it to empty your email trash for you.

IN DEVELOPMENT

WiggleStops

Long drive, little passengers — and somebody always needs a stop just after you pass the exit. WiggleStops will plan the right breaks along your route: playgrounds, parks, food, fuel, and room to wiggle, without sending you miles out of the way.

Built for parents driving three hours or more with kids, it keeps stops on a sensible rhythm — about every two hours — and hands off to your favorite maps app when it's time to roll.

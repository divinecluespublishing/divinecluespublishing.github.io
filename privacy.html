Privacy Policy

Divine Clues Publishing LLC — last updated October 17, 2026.

The short version

We make books and simple, honest apps. We do not sell your information. We do not want your information. Wherever we can, our software does its work on your own device so your personal information never reaches us at all.

Our email cleanup app

  • The app works on your phone. Your email is scanned on your device and is never sent to our servers.
  • We never see, read, store, or sell the contents of your email.
  • The app only changes your mailbox when you tell it to (unsubscribe, block, delete, archive, or empty trash) — and it asks first.
  • Purchases and donations are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your credit card.
  • The app keeps a record on your device of your purchase or donation so it knows you are a supporter. That record stays on your device.

Sweep My Email and your Google account. When you connect a Gmail account, Sweep My Email asks Google for permission to tidy your mailbox, and it does all of its work on your phone. To show you who emails you most, it reads message headers only — who sent it, the subject, the date, and any unsubscribe link — never the body of your messages. It changes your mailbox only when you tap a button: unsubscribe, block, delete, archive, or empty the trash, and it asks before emptying the trash. Sign-in tokens stay in the app's memory and are never written down or sent anywhere except back to Google. Nothing about your mail — not the headers, not the counts — is ever sent to us or anyone else; we run no server for it to be sent to. You can cut the app off at any time from your Google Account's "Third-party access" page, and deleting the app removes everything it keeps on your phone. Sweep My Email's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This is a simple information website. It does not run advertising trackers. If you email us, we will read your message and reply; we will not add you to a marketing list without your permission.

Books

Our books are sold through Amazon. Purchases there are covered by Amazon's own privacy policy.

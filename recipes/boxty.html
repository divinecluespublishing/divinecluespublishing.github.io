In Tara's Kitchen

Boxty

Serves 4 (about 8 pancakes)  ·  Prep 20 min  ·  Cook 20 min

Half grated potato, half mash, all crisp edges — the pancake Ireland argues about. The whole secret is squeezing the grated raw potato dry before it ever meets the bowl. Every county swears theirs is the true boxty; in my kitchen the true one is whichever pile disappears first.

Ingredients

  • 250 g (9 oz) raw potatoes, peeled and coarsely grated
  • 250 g (9 oz) cold mashed potato
  • 150 g (1¼ cups) plain flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp fine sea salt
  • 150–200 ml (⅔–¾ cup) milk
  • Butter, for frying

Method

  1. Wrap the grated potato in a clean tea towel or cheesecloth and squeeze hard over the sink until no more liquid comes out. This is the whole secret — do not skip it.
  2. Tip the squeezed potato into a bowl with the mashed potato and loosen it with a fork.
  3. Stir in the flour, baking powder and salt, then enough milk to make a thick, dropping batter.
  4. Melt butter in a heavy frying pan or griddle over medium heat.
  5. Fry heaped spoonfuls of batter, flattened slightly, for 3–4 minutes a side, until deep golden with lacy, crisp edges.
  6. Keep warm in a low oven while you fry the rest. Serve at once, with butter.

From the pantry

  • A sturdy box grater
  • Cheesecloth — for squeezing the potato dry
  • A cast iron skillet — boxty's best friend

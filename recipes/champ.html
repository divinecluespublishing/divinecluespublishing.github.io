In Tara's Kitchen

Champ

Serves 4–6  ·  Prep 10 min  ·  Cook 20 min

Champ is colcannon's gentler sister: potatoes mashed silky with hot scallion milk and served with a well of butter melting down through the middle. You dip every forkful through the butter as you go — that is not a serving suggestion, it is the law of champ. Poetry is fine, but can poetry do this?

Ingredients

  • 1 kg (2¼ lb) floury potatoes, peeled and quartered
  • 1 bunch scallions (spring onions), 6–8, finely chopped
  • 150 ml (⅔ cup) milk
  • 75 g (5 tbsp) butter, plus extra for the well
  • Fine sea salt and white or black pepper

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water for about 20 minutes, until completely tender.
  2. Meanwhile, put the milk and scallions in a small pan, bring just to a simmer, then set aside to infuse.
  3. Drain the potatoes well and let them steam dry in the hot pot for a minute.
  4. Mash with the butter, then beat in the hot scallion milk until silky and soft.
  5. Season generously. Serve in warm bowls with a well in the centre of each, holding a knob of butter to melt down through.

From the pantry

  • A potato ricer — champ should be silky
  • A proper butter dish
  • Flaky sea salt
